THE AUTHOR'S ROADMAP

The 8 Steps of Making a Book

Every finished book has travelled a journey. Here's yours.

01
💡
START HERE

Find Your Idea

Every book starts with an idea. It could be a question, a character, a strange situation, a dream, or simply the thought: "What if...?"

💡 Quick Trick: Write down ten "What if?" questions. Your favourite one might become your book.
Try the Idea Generator →
02
🧑‍🚀
BRING THEM TO LIFE

Create Characters

Characters are the people readers follow through your story. Give them goals, fears, strengths, weaknesses and personalities.

🧠 Remember: A character becomes interesting when they want something and something stands in their way.
Build a Character →
03
🌎
CREATE THE SETTING

Build Your World

Decide where your story happens. It could be a real city, another planet, an ancient kingdom, your school or a completely imaginary world.

🌎 Think about: Place + Time + Atmosphere + Special Rules
Build Your World →
04
🗺️
THE BACKBONE

Plan Your Plot

A plot is the chain of events that makes your story move. A simple structure is: Beginning → Problem → Rising Action → Climax → Ending.

🗺️ Pro Tip: Give your main character a problem they cannot simply walk away from.
Open Plot Planner →
05
✍️
THE MAGIC

Write Your Story

Now turn your plan into scenes and chapters. Don't try to make the first draft perfect. Get the story onto the page first.

✍️ Golden Rule: A messy first draft can become a brilliant final draft. A blank page cannot.
Open Writing Desk →
06
🔍
MAKE IT BETTER

Edit & Revise

Editing is where you improve your story. Look for confusing parts, unnecessary words, grammar mistakes and places where the story slows down.

🔍 Pro Tip: Read your work aloud. You may notice things your eyes skipped over.
Open Editing Checklist →
07
🎨
FIRST IMPRESSION

Design the Cover

Your cover tells potential readers what kind of experience they might find inside.

🎨 Cover Formula: Strong Title + Suitable Mood + Interesting Visual
Design Your Cover →
08
🚀
THE FINISH LINE

Publish & Share

Once your manuscript is ready, you can share it as a school project, printed book, digital book or simply with family and friends.

🚀 Remember: Finishing a book is already a huge achievement.
See Your Book →
